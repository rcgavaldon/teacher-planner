\begin{center}{\Large \textbf{Mixed factoring practice and weekly check}}\\[2pt]{\small Algebra I \textbar\ CK-12: Factoring Completely}\end{center}\vspace{0.25cm}

{\large\textbf{Key Idea}}\par\smallskip
Always try the \textbf{GCF first}, then decide: two terms that are squares → difference of squares; three terms → product-sum trinomial.\par


{\large\textbf{Key Terms}}\par\smallskip
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Factor completely} — keep factoring until nothing else will
\end{itemize}


{\large\textbf{Worked steps}}\par\smallskip
Step 1: Pull out any GCF.\par
Step 2: Match the leftover to a pattern (difference of squares or trinomial) and finish.\par
Example: $2x^2-8=2(x^2-4)=2(x+2)(x-2)$.\par


{\large\textbf{You Try}}\par\smallskip
Factor completely: $3x^2-27$.\par
