\textbf{Vocabulary}\\[5pt]
\textbf{Zero Product Property:} if $A\cdot B=\bl[0.9cm]{0}$, then $A=\bl[0.9cm]{0}$ or $B=\bl[0.9cm]{0}$.\\[10pt]
\textbf{Root (solution):} an $x$ that makes the equation \bl[2.6cm]{true}; on a graph it is an \bl[2.8cm]{x-intercept}.\\[14pt]
\textbf{We do together.}\quad Solve $x^{2}+5x+6=0$.\\[6pt]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*.},leftmargin=3.1em,itemsep=9pt]
\item One side already $=0$. Good.
\item Factor: $x^{2}+5x+6=\big(\bl[1.8cm]{x+2}\big)\big(\bl[1.8cm]{x+3}\big)$
\item Set each factor $=0$: \quad $\bl[2.2cm]{x+2}=0$ \quad or \quad $\bl[2.2cm]{x+3}=0$
\item Solve: \quad $x=\bl[1.3cm]{-2}$ \quad or \quad $x=\bl[1.3cm]{-3}$
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{See it:} the roots are where the parabola crosses the $x$-axis.
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
\begin{axis}[axis lines=middle,xlabel=$x$,ylabel=$y$,xmin=-5.5,xmax=1,ymin=-1,ymax=6.5,
 width=10cm,height=5.2cm,grid=both,xtick={-5,-4,-3,-2,-1,0},ytick={2,4,6},samples=80]

\end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{You Try.}\ Solve each. Show every step, then name the roots.\\[6pt]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*)},leftmargin=2.4em,itemsep=1.4cm]
\item $x^{2}-x-12=0$ \hfill \ifkey\textcolor{blue!65!black}{$(x-4)(x+3)=0\Rightarrow x=4,\,-3$}\fi
\item $x^{2}-9=0$ \hfill \ifkey\textcolor{blue!65!black}{$(x-3)(x+3)=0\Rightarrow x=3,\,-3$}\fi
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{Summary (fill in):} To solve a quadratic by factoring: make one side $=\bl[0.8cm]{0}$, \bl[2.2cm]{factor} the other side, then use the \bl[3.6cm]{Zero Product Property} and solve each factor.
