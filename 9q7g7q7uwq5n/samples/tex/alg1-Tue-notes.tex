\begin{center}{\Large \textbf{Factor trinomials of the form x\textasciicircum{}2 + bx + c}}\\[2pt]{\small Algebra I \textbar\ CK-12: Factoring when the Leading Coefficient Equals 1}\end{center}\vspace{0.25cm}

{\large\textbf{Key Idea}}\par\smallskip
To factor $x^2+bx+c$, find two numbers that \textbf{multiply to $c$} and \textbf{add to $b$}. Those numbers go into $(x+\_)(x+\_)$.\par


{\large\textbf{Key Terms}}\par\smallskip
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Trinomial} — a three-term expression
  \item \textbf{Product-sum} — multiply to $c$, add to $b$
\end{itemize}


{\large\textbf{Worked steps}}\par\smallskip
Step 1: List factor pairs of $c$.\par
Step 2: Pick the pair that adds to $b$; place them in $(x+\_)(x+\_)$.\par
Example: $x^2+5x+6$: $2\cdot3=6$ and $2+3=5$, so $x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$.\par


{\large\textbf{You Try}}\par\smallskip
Factor $x^2+7x+10$.\par
