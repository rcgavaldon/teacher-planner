Phones

Phones go up at the start — every day. Think of the movies: they ask you to put your phone away so everyone can focus. Same here. I’ll ask once. After that it’s an email home, then a meeting with you, your parent, and admin — my goal is to never need that.

Tutoring & not failing

Fail a test → one week of mandatory tutoring (the tutoring department or me, on tutoring days). Tutoring is how we keep you from failing — that’s the whole point. Seats are assigned; I set the chart.

EXPECT

What I expect

Quality of Work means

  • You finish your work on your own.
  • Classwork gets done in class, the day it’s assigned.
  • Still on THIS class? Don’t work on other classes or activities.
  • No AI in class, and no AI on tests. Learn with it outside class if you want — tests are AI‑free, so you have to actually know it.

Not accepted

  • Yelling, fighting, hitting or touching others
  • Eating in class, cussing, out of uniform
  • Not turning in work, or not working during class
  • No‑showing your tutoring
  • Popping up to move tables, or asking for the restroom every single day
