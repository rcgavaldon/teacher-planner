\textbf{Vocabulary}\\[5pt]
\textbf{Translation:} a \bl[1.6cm]{slide} of the graph --- $f(x)+k$ moves it \bl[2.2cm]{up/down}, and $f(x-h)$ moves it \bl[2.4cm]{left/right}.\\[10pt]
\textbf{Reflection:} a flip: $-f(x)$ flips over the \bl[1.8cm]{$x$-axis}, and $f(-x)$ flips over the \bl[1.8cm]{$y$-axis}.\\[12pt]
\textbf{We do together.}\quad Describe how $g(x)=(x-3)^{2}+2$ transforms the parent $x^{2}$.\\[6pt]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*.},leftmargin=3.1em,itemsep=9pt]
  \item Inside the square, $x-3$ shifts the graph \bl[1.8cm]{right 3} (opposite the sign).
  \item The $+2$ outside shifts it \bl[1.5cm]{up 2}.
  \item So the vertex moves from $(0,0)$ to \bl[1.8cm]{$(3,2)$}.
\end{enumerate}\vspace{8pt}

{\small\centering parent (dashed) $\to$ right 3, up 2 (solid).\par}

  \item Describe $h(x)=-x^{2}$ from the parent. \hfill \ifkey\textcolor{blue!65!black}{reflection over the $x$-axis (opens down)}\fi

\vspace{4pt}\hrule\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{Summary (fill in):} $f(x-h)+k$ shifts a graph \bl[3cm]{$h$ right, $k$ up}; a leading $-f(x)$ \bl[2cm]{reflects} it over the $x$-axis.
