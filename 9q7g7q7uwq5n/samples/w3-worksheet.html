Use the two shift rules: outside moves as written, inside moves opposite. Domains and ranges slide with the graph.

Type 1 Describe the shift
Example (Describe the shift)

$y=(x-3)^{2}+2$ — inside $x-3$ moves RIGHT 3, outside $+2$ moves UP 2. The vertex lands at $(3,2)$.

y = x² (indigo) vs y = (x−3)² + 2 (gold)
