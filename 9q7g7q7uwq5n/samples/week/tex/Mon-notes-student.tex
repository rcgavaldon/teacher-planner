\textbf{Vocabulary}\\[5pt]
\textbf{Parent function:} the \bl[2cm]{simplest} form of a family --- no shifts, stretches, or reflections.\\[10pt]
\textbf{Domain / Range:} domain is the set of allowed \bl[1.2cm]{$x$}-values; range is the set of resulting \bl[1.2cm]{$y$}-values.\\[12pt]
\textbf{We do together.}\quad Name the parent of $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and give its domain.\\[6pt]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*.},leftmargin=3.1em,itemsep=9pt]
  \item Match the rule to a known family: this is the \bl[3cm]{square root} parent.
  \item Recall its shape: it starts at the point \bl[1.8cm]{$(0,0)$} and curves up to the right.
  \item A square root needs a non-negative inside, so the domain is \bl[2cm]{$x\ge 0$}.
\end{enumerate}\vspace{8pt}
\textbf{See it:}

  \item Name the parent of $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and give its domain. \hfill \ifkey\textcolor{blue!65!black}{reciprocal (rational); domain $x\ne 0$}\fi

\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Summary (fill in):} To name a parent: match the \bl[1.5cm]{rule} to a family, recall its \bl[1.6cm]{shape}, then state the \bl[1.8cm]{domain}.
