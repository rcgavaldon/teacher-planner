\textbf{Vocabulary}\\[5pt]
\textbf{Parent Function:} The simplest function in a family; for quadratics this is \bl[2.5cm]{$y=x^2$}.\\[10pt]
\textbf{Reflection:} A flip of the graph over an axis; a \bl[2cm]{negative} value of $a$ reflects $y=x^2$ over the x-axis.\\[10pt]
\textbf{Vertical Stretch/Compression:} When $|a|>1$ the graph becomes \bl[2.5cm]{narrower}; when $0<|a|<1$ the graph becomes \bl[2cm]{wider}.\\[12pt]
\textbf{We do together.}\quad Describe the transformations from $y=x^2$ to $y=-2(x+1)^2+3$.\\[6pt]
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Step \arabic*.},leftmargin=3.1em,itemsep=9pt]
  \item The term $(x+1)$ shifts the graph \bl[1.5cm]{left} by \bl[1cm]{$1$} unit.
  \item The negative sign in front of the 2 causes a \bl[2.5cm]{reflection} over the x-axis.
  \item Since $|a|=2>1$, the graph becomes \bl[2.5cm]{narrower} than $y=x^2$.
  \item The $+3$ shifts the graph \bl[1.5cm]{up} by \bl[1cm]{$3$} units.
  \item Final vertex: \bl[2.5cm]{$(-1,3)$}.
\end{enumerate}\vspace{8pt}
