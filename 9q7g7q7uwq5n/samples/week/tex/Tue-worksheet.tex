\textbf{Procedure:} Read the inside $(x-h)$: shift opposite the sign (left/right); Read the outside $+k$: shift up/down; A leading $-$ reflects over the $x$-axis; Vertex lands at $(h,k)$.\\[8pt]

  \item Up 3; vertex $(0,3)$
  \item Left 5, down 2; vertex $(-5,-2)$
  \item Right 1, reflect over $x$-axis; vertex $(1,0)$, opens down
  \item Right 3, up 4; vertex $(3,4)$
  \item Down 6; vertex $(0,-6)$
  \item Left 2; vertex $(-2,0)$
  \item Left 4, up 1, reflect over $x$-axis; vertex $(-4,1)$, opens down
